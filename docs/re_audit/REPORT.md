## Mathematical interpretation and tests that cannot certify research

For a declared sequence, ordinary admitted updates satisfy `y_n=(1-beta_n)y_(n-1)+beta_n*x_n`, where `beta=k*alpha`. With first-value initialization, the first admitted event instead assigns `y=x`; its effective beta is one. With zero initialization the ordinary formula applies immediately. Those conventions must never be silently exchanged to improve a transient plot. The new field makes the distinction observable.

Convexity proves bounded ideal-real-arithmetic state for bounded input and a declared finite initial state. Conditional forgetting is the product of `1-beta`; an infinite run of holds does not forget its initial condition. If feedback produces different masks/coefficients on different trajectories, a common-sequence contraction argument is not an end-to-end feedback-system proof. Tiny coefficients and extreme ranges also have finite-precision limits; an analytic cutoff is not a measurement of the actual adaptive run.

A frozen EMA has nominal `H(z)=alpha/[1-(1-alpha)z^-1]`; its DC group delay is `(1-alpha)/alpha` and its half-power crossing satisfies `sin(w/2)=alpha/[2*sqrt(1-alpha)]`. Those are fixed-coefficient, regular-event-clock formulas. They do not attach physical Hz, a single transfer function, or an automatically matched baseline to an irregular adaptive/admitted stream. Butterworth similarly requires a declared uniform grid and a causal transformation. [SciPy Butterworth documentation](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.signal.butter.html).

A same-alpha full-rate reference and held admitted state obey `d_n=(1-k_n*alpha_n)d_(n-1)+(1-k_n)*alpha_n*(r_(n-1)-x_n)` after aligned startup. This explains skipped-state error, but method-specific references are not a common task-quality denominator. An independent scientific target must decide what useful error means. Agreement with a chosen EMA can validly assess approximation to that EMA; it cannot establish that this EMA is a universally optimal signal or natural ground truth.

ROC/AP tests cover exact grouped ties and score ordering; they do not certify label ontology, causal preprocessing or test-set independence. Event matching is explicitly forward greedy one-to-one; overlapping windows may require another preregistered task policy. Fully observed recall is a separate statistic, not permission to erase censored events. Paired t intervals require independent units and a justified distribution/mean approximation; sign flips require the stated symmetry/exchangeability null. Bootstrap seeds, exact permutation resolution and degenerate variance do not manufacture independent source units.

## Direction, remaining blockers and handoff

Continuing as a falsifiable study of **whether load-driven coefficient adaptation adds useful value beyond admission** is defensible. Novelty and publishability are unestablished. A narrow negative result may be useful if the comparison, uncertainty, scope and mechanism are credible; nonsignificance alone is not equivalence. Engineering corrections and passing tests are not themselves a journal contribution.

The project still lacks a human-justified primary quality objective and practical margin; a DSP/streaming factory profile; actual bounded runtime/sink and causal load trace; current-task baseline implementations/tuning budgets; prospective development/confirmatory population and dependence analysis; a measured M3 pilot; registered statistical selection/multiplicity/censoring rules; and a completed claim-to-evidence reproduction package. No cohort, natural market labels, training history or physical measurements were invented to fill these gaps. The user was asked about the quality objective; no answer is assumed in the scientific plan.

The full implementation program remains in root `plan.md`; its October addendum binds these new findings and acceptance tests to C01–C14. `factory/LOCAL_AUDIT_POLICY.md` is the compatibility policy for the modified local factory. The initial migration manifests/logs remain historical rather than being overwritten as though they validated current bytes. `verify_reaudit.py`, its current inventory and verification record bind the current files and checks. Read the coverage/scope fields before interpreting any PASS.
